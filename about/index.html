Meet The Sugarist

About Us

I'm Rebecca, a Las Vegas Sugaring Specialist.

I've been a licensed Esthetician since 2018. I started learning the art of Sugaring Hair Removal 3 years ago and I can't see myself doing anything else. Aside from focusing on the skin goals of my clients, I aim to create an atmosphere of comfort and ease. Any of my clients will tell you that I'm not actually 'working' at my salon, I'm just chatting with friends while I remove their hair. You'll find me laughing, talking about my kids, asking about YOU and just wondering out loud about the world we live in. Meeting clients and learning their skin care goals is not just part of the process, it's part of what makes me love my job. Getting rid of your ingrowns is just the cherry on top.
