How long does a lash lift and tint last?

A lash lift and tint generally lasts six to eight weeks, which mirrors the natural growth and shed cycle of your eyelashes. As old lashes drop out naturally, new untreated lashes grow in, slowly relaxing the lifted curl over a two-month period.

The tint color typically remains rich and noticeable for four to five weeks before fading slightly. To maximize the longevity of your lash lift and tint, keep your eyelashes completely dry for the first 24 hours after treatment. Avoid steam, heavy sweat, oil-based eye makeup removers, and waterproof mascara, as heavy oils break down the perming lifting solution and tint pigments faster than normal, causing premature curl loss. Routine maintenance every two months ensures your eyelashes remain consistently curled, dark, and beautifully framed.
