How do I remove nose hair?

The safest way to remove visible nose hair is using an electric nose hair trimmer or rounded safety scissors to trim long hairs extending past the nostrils. Trimming controls visible hair safely without damaging the delicate, sensitive lining inside your nasal passages.

For longer-lasting smooth results, professional nose waxing or sugaring removes hair from the front edge of the nostril opening. An esthetician applies warm wax or sugar paste to a small applicator stick, inserts it slightly into the lower nostril, and pulls it out quickly. Never remove hair deep inside the nose, as internal cilia protect your respiratory system from dust, pollen, and airborne particles. Professional waxing or sugaring provides a clean, controlled finish around the nostril border while keeping your airways fully protected.
