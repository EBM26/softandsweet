How often should you get your eyebrows waxed?

You should get your eyebrows waxed or sugared every three to four weeks. This timeframe matches natural eyebrow hair growth cycles, allowing stray hairs to grow long enough for wax or sugar paste to grab them cleanly from the root without breaking.

Booking appointments every three to four weeks keeps your brow arches defined and neat without requiring daily tweezing. Avoid plucking stray hairs between visits, as tweezing disrupts natural hair growth phases, leading to uneven regrowth at your next brow appointment. Consistent professional maintenance trains brow hair to grow back sparser, finer, and much cleaner over time between scheduled visits. Keeping up with regular visits makes each session quicker and helps your brows retain their clean shape effortlessly. Consistent eyebrow grooming keeps your arch shape looking clean and well-defined.
