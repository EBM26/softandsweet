What is a lash lift and tint?

A lash lift and tint is a low-maintenance professional eyelash treatment that accentuates your natural lashes without requiring extensions. A lash lift acts like a perm for your eyelashes, using a safe chemical solution to curl and lift your natural lashes upward from the root over a silicone shield, creating the appearance of longer, fuller lashes.

The tinting portion applies a semi-permanent vegetable dye to your lashes, darkening light tips and giving the appearance of fresh mascara. The combined treatment opens up your eyes instantly, giving you dark, lifted, and fluttery lashes that require zero daily curling or mascara application. Results look beautifully defined while keeping your morning routine quick and effortless. It is an ideal service for anyone seeking effortless daily beauty without the hassle of artificial lash maintenance.
