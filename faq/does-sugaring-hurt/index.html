Does sugaring hurt?

Sugaring causes some physical discomfort because hair is pulled directly from the root, but most people find it significantly less painful than traditional waxing. Because sugar paste binds only to dead skin cells and hair—rather than live skin cells—the paste does not yank or pull on your skin during removal.

Additionally, sugaring removes hair in its natural direction of growth. This puts far less strain on the hair follicle, reducing the sharp sting commonly associated with waxing. Discomfort levels decrease with each appointment as hair grows back finer, sparser, and weaker. To minimize discomfort during your session, schedule appointments away from your menstrual cycle, stay well hydrated, and avoid caffeine or alcohol immediately before your scheduled treatment. Taking an over-the-counter pain reliever thirty minutes prior to your appointment can also help take the edge off for first-time clients.
