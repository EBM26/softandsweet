How long does a underarm wax last?

An underarm wax or sugaring session typically lasts three to four weeks before visible hair regrowth begins. Because underarm hair is coarse and dense, pulling it directly from the root keeps armpits completely smooth and stubble-free far longer than daily shaving.

With regular underarm hair removal appointments every four weeks, hair follicles weaken over time. As a result, underarm hair grows back significantly finer, lighter, and sparser with each session. Many clients also notice reduced dark shadow under their arms and smoother skin texture when maintaining a continuous sugaring schedule, eliminating daily razor irritation completely. Switching from daily razors to monthly underarm sugaring or waxing saves time and eliminates painful razor burn completely. Over time, regular underarm appointments result in softer hair growth that is far easier to remove during future salon visits.
