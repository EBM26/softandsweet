What is body sugaring?

Body sugaring is a gentle, all-natural hair removal method designed to remove unwanted body hair from the root using an organic sugar paste. The paste consists purely of sugar, water, and lemon juice, making it completely free of synthetic chemicals, resins, and artificial preservatives.

Estheticians use body sugaring on almost any area of the body, including arms, legs, underarms, chest, back, face, and intimate areas like the bikini line and Brazilian. Because the sugar paste binds only to hair and dead skin cells, it acts as a mild exfoliant while keeping live skin intact. Hair is pulled out in the direction it naturally grows, which reduces hair breakage and minimizes ingrown hairs. Over consistent treatments, body sugaring thins out hair growth, causing hair to grow back softer, finer, and far sparser over time.
