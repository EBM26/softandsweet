What is a brazillian wax?

A Brazilian wax is a complete hair removal service that removes all hair from the pelvic area, including the pubic mound, sides, labia, perineum, and backside area. Clients can choose to remove all hair completely or leave a neat hair strip or square on top.

Traditional Brazilian waxing uses warm hard or soft wax applied to the skin and pulled off rapidly against hair growth. At specialized studios like Soft & Sweet Sugaring, clients often choose Brazilian sugaring instead—using natural sugar paste to achieve the same hair-free results with less skin pulling, zero heat risk, and far fewer ingrown hairs in delicate intimate zones while providing smooth skin. First-time clients can discuss their preferences with their esthetician to customize their hair removal service to their personal comfort level.
