How to exfoliate after brazilian wax?

To exfoliate after a Brazilian wax or sugaring service, wait 48 hours post-appointment to allow open pores and sensitive skin time to calm down completely. Once two days have passed, begin gently exfoliating two to three times per week.

Use a mild, fragrance-free physical scrub, an exfoliating mitt, or a soothing chemical exfoliant containing salicylic or glycolic acid designed for intimate zones. Gently buff the pelvic area in soft circles to prevent dead skin cells from blocking growing hair follicles. Regular post-wax exfoliation is the single best way to stop ingrown hairs and maintain smooth skin between scheduled visits. Establishing a consistent post-wax care routine keeps intimate skin healthy, soft, and completely bump-free between appointments. Staying diligent with gentle exfoliation protects sensitive skin and ensures your intimate areas stay smooth until your next visit.
