How to wax armpits?

To wax underarms successfully, hair should be about a quarter-inch long before starting. Start by cleansing the underarms to remove deodorant, sweat, and oils, then dry skin thoroughly and dust with oil-free powder. Underarm hair grows in multiple directions, so assess the growth pattern carefully before applying wax.

Apply warm wax in the direction of hair growth over one section. Hold underarm skin pulled tight by lifting your elbow high behind your head. Place a cloth strip over the wax, press firmly, and pull the strip quickly in the opposite direction of hair growth, keeping your hand parallel to the skin. Repeat on remaining sections, then soothe skin with aloe vera gel. Practicing proper technique minimizes skin stress and yields completely smooth underarms that stay hair-free for weeks.
