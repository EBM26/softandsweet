How much does it cost to get your eyebrows done?

The cost to get your eyebrows done generally ranges from $15 to $50, depending on the service method, geographic location, and salon skill level. Basic eyebrow threading or waxing typically costs between $15 and $25 at standard beauty salons.

Professional eyebrow shaping, sugaring, or styling at specialized esthetics studios usually runs between $25 and $40. Adding extra eyebrow services like eyebrow tinting or eyebrow lamination increases the total price to $50–$100. At quality studios like Soft & Sweet Sugaring in Spring Valley, NV, clear menu pricing ensures clients receive expert eyebrow mapping, custom shaping, and gentle hair removal tailored to their natural face structure. Investing in professional eyebrow care prevents accidental over-plucking and maintains balanced facial symmetry over time. Choosing an experienced esthetician ensures precise brow shaping and long-lasting results.
