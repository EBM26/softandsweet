What do mud masks do?

Mud masks detoxify, deeply cleanse, and clarify skin by absorbing excess sebum, unclogging pores, and lifting away impurities and dead skin cells. Mineral-rich muds—like Dead Sea mud, Moor mud, or bentonite clay—deliver trace minerals such as magnesium, calcium, and potassium directly to skin tissue.

As a mud mask dries on the skin, it draws out trapped impurities while boosting localized blood circulation. This helps minimize visible pore size, calm active breakouts, balance oily skin, and leave your complexion smooth, refreshed, and toned. Mud masks are particularly beneficial for oily, combination, and acne-prone skin types needing deep clarification without stripping moisture. Incorporating a mud mask into your weekly skincare routine helps maintain balanced oil production and promotes a clear complexion. Regular use of clarifying mud masks keeps pores clear and leaves your skin feeling thoroughly refreshed after every application.
