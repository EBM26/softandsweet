Where did sugaring originate from?

Sugaring is one of the oldest recorded hair removal methods in history, originating in ancient Egypt and the Middle East around 1900 BC. Ancient Egyptian women considered hairless skin a symbol of personal purity, hygiene, and high social standing.

Historically, women prepared honey and sugar mixtures at home to remove body hair smoothly without modern tools. The practice spread across North Africa and the Mediterranean, becoming a traditional grooming ritual for brides and daily hygiene routines across generations. Modern body sugaring uses the exact same foundational recipe—sugar, lemon juice, and water—refined into a professional esthetics treatment practiced in modern skin salons worldwide to deliver gentle, long-lasting hair removal without synthetic chemicals. Today, both men and women choose sugaring as a natural, time-tested alternative to synthetic waxing products. Traditional sugaring techniques have passed down through generations of estheticians worldwide as an effective hair removal solution.
