How long will a leg wax last?

A full or half leg wax typically lasts three to six weeks before you need another hair removal appointment. Because waxing removes leg hair directly from the root, skin stays smooth and stubble-free far longer than daily shaving.

Initial regrowth usually appears around week three as fine hairs in different growth phases surface naturally. By keeping a consistent waxing or sugaring routine every four to five weeks, hair follicles weaken over time. This results in much sparser, finer leg hair that takes longer to grow back, allowing you to enjoy smooth legs for up to six full weeks between visits without daily razor maintenance. Enjoying hair-free legs for weeks at a time makes leg waxing an exceptionally convenient option for busy schedules and travel.
