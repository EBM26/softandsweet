Can sugaring cause yeast infections?

Sugaring itself does not cause yeast infections. Sugar paste contains only sugar, water, and lemon juice, and is completely water-soluble, meaning any residue rinses away easily with plain water during treatment. The paste does not enter the vaginal canal or disrupt internal vaginal flora.

However, improper post-care can create conditions where yeast thrives. Removing hair opens hair follicles temporarily. Wearing tight synthetic clothing, working out immediately after an appointment, or sitting in sweaty clothes traps heat and moisture around the pelvic area, encouraging yeast growth. To prevent issues, wear loose cotton underwear and keep the area clean and dry for 24 hours post-sugaring to support healthy skin recovery. Choosing breathable cotton fabrics and avoiding hot baths immediately after your appointment helps maintain optimal personal hygiene.
