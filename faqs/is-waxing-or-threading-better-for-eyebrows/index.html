Is waxing or threading better for eyebrows?

Whether waxing or threading is better for eyebrows comes down to your skin sensitivity, brow goals, and personal preference. Threading uses a twisted cotton thread to pluck individual hairs quickly without touching the skin, making it ideal for people taking retinoids or living with sensitive skin.

Waxing removes multiple hairs simultaneously, making it faster and great for thick brows, though it pulls slightly on delicate eye skin. Body sugaring offers a fantastic middle ground: it provides the speed of waxing while sticking only to hair shafts rather than live skin cells, minimizing redness, discomfort, and skin irritation around the eyebrows for clean, defined arches. Consulting with a knowledgeable esthetician helps you choose the most effective hair removal method for your unique skin type.
