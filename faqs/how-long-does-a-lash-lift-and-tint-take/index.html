How long does a lash lift and tint take?

A professional lash lift and tint appointment typically takes 45 to 60 minutes from start to finish. The process is relaxing, comfortable, and completely painless, requiring you to keep your eyes closed throughout the service while resting comfortably on a treatment table.

During the appointment, the esthetician spends 10 minutes cleansing lashes and positioning custom silicone shields on your eyelids. The lifting perming solution sets for 10 to 15 minutes, followed by a setting lotion for another 10 minutes. Applying and processing the lash tint takes about 10 minutes, with the remaining time spent applying a nourishing conditioning serum to strengthen and hydrate your lifted lashes before you leave. You can easily fit a lash lift and tint appointment into a lunch break or afternoon wellness routine.
