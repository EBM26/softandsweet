Should I exfoliate before waxing?

Yes, you should exfoliate your skin 24 to 48 hours before your waxing or sugaring appointment. Gentle pre-wax exfoliation removes dead skin cells surrounding hair follicles, allowing the wax or sugar paste to grip short hairs cleanly from the root.

Exfoliating before your service also reduces the risk of ingrown hairs and makes hair removal less painful. However, do not exfoliate on the day of your appointment, as fresh exfoliation can make skin overly sensitive to wax or sugar paste. Skip harsh scrubs immediately after your treatment as well, waiting 48 hours before resuming regular exfoliation routines for optimal skin healing. Taking time to prepare your skin properly guarantees a smoother, more comfortable hair removal experience from start to finish.
