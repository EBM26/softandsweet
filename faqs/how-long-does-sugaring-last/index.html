How long does sugaring last?

Sugaring results typically last between three to six weeks, depending on your individual hair growth cycle and how consistently you receive treatments. Because sugaring pulls hair out directly from the root, the hair follicle must rebuild completely before new hair becomes visible at the skin surface.

During your first few sessions, you might notice light regrowth around three weeks as hair sitting in different growth phases emerges naturally. However, with regular appointments spaced four weeks apart, hair follicles weaken over time. This leads to noticeably slower, thinner, and softer regrowth across all treated areas. Many clients who maintain a routine sugaring schedule find they can comfortably extend the time between appointments to five or six weeks while enjoying long-lasting smooth, hair-free skin.
