How to get rid of hair on the upper lip?

You can remove upper lip hair using several methods depending on your desired results and skin sensitivity. Shaving or dermaplaning offers quick, painless removal at the skin surface, but hair returns within days. Depilatory creams dissolve surface hair chemically, though they can irritate sensitive facial skin.

For long-lasting results lasting three to four weeks, professional sugaring, waxing, or threading removes upper lip hair directly from the root. Sugaring is particularly recommended for the upper lip because sugar paste sticks only to hair, preventing skin lifting or redness. Permanent options include laser hair removal or electrolysis for dark or stubborn facial hairs seeking permanent reduction. Discussing your options with a skin specialist ensures you pick the best hair removal approach for your skin type.
